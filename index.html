Comprehensive Analysis of Electric Vehicle Market

This project, in collaboration with Ford Motors, aimed to analyze the current landscape and evolution of the electric vehicle (EV) market, focusing on Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs).
